30-Day Lunch Break Challenge

Thirty days, one lunch break each. 25 to 35 minutes, dumbbells or just bodyweight. Five training days a week, easy days on the weekend. Each week adds a little.

How to use it: do the day that matches, at the same time each day. Tap a day to open it. If you miss one, just do today's. Write your weights down on Day 1; you'll need them on Day 29.

Safety: this is general fitness info for healthy adults, not medical advice. If something hurts (not just hard, actually hurts), stop and check with a doctor.

Week 1

Day 1Upper body: push and pull25 min
  • Warm-up (4 min): 10 bodyweight squats, 10 arm circles each way, 8 inchworms, 20 sec plank.
  • A1. Dumbbell floor press: 2 x 8-10
  • A2. One-arm dumbbell row: 2 x 8-10 each side. Rest 75 sec after both.
  • B1. Seated dumbbell shoulder press: 2 x 8-10
  • B2. Band pull-apart: 2 x 15 (no band: prone Y raise x 10). Rest 75 sec.
  • C. Push-ups: 2 sets, stop 2 reps before you can't. Elevate your hands on a desk if needed.
Pick a weight you could lift 2-3 more times. That's the right weight.
Day 2Lower body25 min
  • Warm-up (4 min): 10 bodyweight squats, 10 arm circles each way, 8 inchworms, 20 sec plank.
  • A. Goblet squat: 2 x 10. Rest 75 sec.
  • B. Dumbbell Romanian deadlift: 2 x 10. Rest 75 sec.
  • C1. Reverse lunge: 2 x 8 each leg
  • C2. Glute bridge: 2 x 12. Rest 60 sec after both.
  • D. Farmer carry: 2 x 40 steps, heavy as you can hold with good posture.
Sit down between your heels on the squat. Chest up, slow on the way down.
Day 3Easy day: walk + mobility20 min
  • Brisk walk, 15 minutes. Phone in your pocket.
  • Then 5 minutes: hip flexor stretch 30 sec each side, doorway chest stretch 30 sec, cat-cow x 10, child's pose 60 sec.
Easy days are part of the plan. They're why the hard days keep working.
Day 4Full-body circuit24 min
  • Warm-up (4 min): 10 bodyweight squats, 10 arm circles each way, 8 inchworms, 20 sec plank.
  • 3 rounds, 30 sec work / 30 sec rest:
  • 1. Dumbbell thruster (squat into a press)
  • 2. Push-ups (hands on a desk if needed)
  • 3. Bent-over dumbbell row
  • 4. Reverse lunge, alternating legs
  • 5. Dead bug
  • Rest 1 minute between rounds.
Light weights. This one is about moving well and breathing hard, not max effort.
Day 5Conditioning + core22 min
  • Warm-up (4 min): 10 bodyweight squats, 10 arm circles each way, 8 inchworms, 20 sec plank.
  • EMOM for 12 minutes (start a new set at the top of every minute, rest the remainder):
  • Odd minutes: 8 goblet squats + 6 push-ups
  • Even minutes: 10 mountain climbers each side + 8 dumbbell swings (or 10 jump squats)
  • Core finisher, 2 rounds: 30 sec plank, 20 sec side plank each side, 10 bird dogs each side.
If you can't finish inside the minute, cut the reps. Don't skip the rest.
