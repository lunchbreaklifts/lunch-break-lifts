Privacy Policy

Lunch Break Lifts (@lunch_breaklifts) and its publishing app "LBL Publisher". Last updated November 2, 2026.

What this is

LBL Publisher is a private tool used only by the Lunch Break Lifts account to publish its own posts to Instagram, Facebook and Threads, reply to comments on its own posts, and send the free 30-day plan by direct message to people who ask for it. It is not offered to other people or businesses.

What we access

  • Comments left on Lunch Break Lifts posts (the comment text, your username, and when it was posted), so we can reply.
  • When you comment the keyword for the free plan, we send one direct message to you with a link to the plan.
  • Basic stats about our own posts (views, likes, saves) to decide what to post next.

We do not access your private profile information, contacts, location, or anything outside our own posts and conversations.

How we use and keep it

  • Comment text may be read by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to write a reply. It is not used to train models.
  • We keep a short log of recent comments for up to 30 days so we don't reply twice. People who requested the plan are stored only as a scrambled code, not their username.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your information, and we don't run ads based on it.

Deleting your data

Delete your comment on Instagram and we will no longer see it. To ask us to delete anything else, send a direct message to @lunch_breaklifts on Instagram with the word "delete" and we will remove it within 30 days.

Contact
