# Privacy Policy

_Lunch Break Lifts (@lunch_breaklifts) and its publishing app "LBL Publisher". Last updated November 3, 2026._

## What this is

LBL Publisher is a private tool used only by the Lunch Break Lifts account. It publishes the page's own
posts to Instagram, Facebook, Threads and YouTube, replies to comments on the page's own Instagram posts,
and sends the free 30-day plan by direct message to people who ask for it. It is not offered to other
people or businesses.

## Automation, in plain words

- Comment replies and the plan message are written and sent automatically. Comments the tool isn't sure
  about (health or injury questions, business offers, personal details, or anyone asking whether replies
  are automated) are left for the page owner to answer personally.
- The page's posts are written with the help of an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) and use licensed stock
  footage and music.

## What we access

- Comments left on Lunch Break Lifts posts (the comment text, your username, and when it was posted), so
  we can reply.
- When you comment the keyword for the free plan, we send you one direct message with a link to the plan.
  That's the only message you'll get from us.
- Basic stats about our own posts (views, likes, saves) to decide what to post next.

We do not access your private profile information, contacts, location, or anything outside our own posts
and conversations.

## How we use and keep it

- Comment text may be read by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to write a reply. It is not used to train models.
- We don't save your username or your comment. We keep only the comment's ID and a one-way code
  (which can't be turned back into your name) for up to 30 days, so we don't reply twice. If you asked for
  the plan, that code is kept so we never message you twice.
- If a common question from the comments is answered in a post, it's answered anonymously. We never name or
  tag you in a post without asking first.
- We never sell, rent, or share your information, and we don't run ads based on it.

## Who it's for

Our content is general fitness information for healthy adults, not medical advice.

## Deleting your data, or being left alone

Delete your comment on Instagram and we will no longer see it. To have us delete anything else, or to make
sure the tool never replies to or messages you again, send a direct message to
[@lunch_breaklifts](https://www.instagram.com/lunch_breaklifts/) on Instagram with the word "delete". We'll
take care of it within 30 days.
