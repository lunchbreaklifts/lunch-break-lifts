Desk job wrist stretches you can do between meetings. Takes under a minute and nobody notices. Hold your arms out, make big slow circles one way, then the other. 10 each. Do them before you start typing again, not after your wrists already feel cooked. If anything feels sharp, stop and skip it. Which hand does most of your mousing, left or right?
