A no sweat workout you can do at work in your regular clothes.

All you need is a resistance band and a little floor space, like an empty conference room. Wrap the band behind your back for presses. Pull it apart at chest height for back flyes. Loop it above your knees and walk sideways for monster walks. Slow and controlled keeps your heart rate down and your shirt dry.
