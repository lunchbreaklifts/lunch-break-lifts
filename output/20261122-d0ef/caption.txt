A beginner core workout tip for the dead bug.

The point is keeping your low back on the floor while your arm and leg move. Once it peels up, the rep stops counting. Don't reach your leg so far out. Bend the knee more, move slower, and breathe out as you reach. Shorter and clean beats long and arched.
