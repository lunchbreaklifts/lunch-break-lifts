A lunch workout fails most often because of the minutes around the lifting, not the lifting itself. Changing, finding a spot, deciding what to do, getting lunch. Plan those and the training part gets easy. Tweak the times to fit your commute to the gym or your office setup. What eats the most time in your lunch break right now?
