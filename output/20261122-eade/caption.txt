A calf raise tip for your lunch break workout.

Bouncing lets your tendons do the work and your calves sit it out. Hold a dumbbell, go up high on your toes, hold for two full seconds, then lower slowly all the way down. You'll want lighter weight than you think. That's normal.

At the end of leg day, try 3 sets of 12 this way.
