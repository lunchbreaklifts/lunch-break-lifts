Desk job shoulder exercises don't need heavy weight. External rotations work best with the lightest pair in the rack, not the one you press with. Keep your elbow against your side, turn your hand out, and take a full second coming back. Two sets of 12 makes a decent warm-up before pressing. Which shoulder feels worse, left or right?
