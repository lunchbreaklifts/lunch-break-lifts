Stretches for stiff hips between meetings, part 2.

After three calls in a row your hips feel glued to the chair. Stand up, hands on your hips, and make big slow circles, 10 one way and 10 the other. Then feet together, hands on your knees, and circle them 10 each way. Under two minutes, done in work clothes.

Pair it with the neck one from part 1 and you've got a reset you can do anywhere.
