Bodyweight workout at the gym or office with just a pull-up bar and a sturdy step. Pair the pull-ups with push-ups so you're not standing around between sets. If you can't get 5 pull-ups yet, do fewer and lower slowly on the way down. Stop each set with one or two reps left. You don't need to grind. Want a version with a band for the pull-ups?
