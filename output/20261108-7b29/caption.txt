A 25 minute dumbbell workout for when your morning ran long and lunch got cut short.

One pair of dumbbells covers it. Pair the moves so you rest from one while doing the other. Skip the curls and the extra ab stuff today, you don't have the time and you don't need it.

Pick a weight where the last 2 reps feel hard but clean. If your dumbbells are heavy enough for the rows, they're probably too heavy for the spell casters. Go lighter there.
