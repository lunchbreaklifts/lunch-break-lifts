How to get better at pull ups when you only train on your lunch break.

Half reps feel productive because the number goes up. But if you stop at your nose and never fully straighten your arms at the bottom, you're skipping the hardest part of the rep.

Start from a dead hang every rep. Pull your elbows down toward your back pockets. If that drops you from 8 to 3, good, that's your real number now. Do a few sets of those.
