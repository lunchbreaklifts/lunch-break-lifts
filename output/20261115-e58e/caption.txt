Dumbbell squat form fix for anyone whose squats stop halfway. Most of the time it's not your legs. You're sitting back like there's a chair behind you, so you lose balance early. Hold one dumbbell at your chest, feet a bit wider than your hips, and sit straight down between your heels. Chest tall. Go as low as you can while your heels stay down.
