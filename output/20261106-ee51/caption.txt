Workout in work clothes is totally doable if you pick the right move. Slow step-ups with a knee raise keep your pants happy and your shirt dry. Use a sturdy bench or a low stair, drive the knee up at the top, and take your time coming down. Do 10 each side for a few rounds. Save the jumping and deep lunges for gym clothes.
