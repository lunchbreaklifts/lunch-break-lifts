A no-sweat lunch workout is mostly about pace.

Jumping and short rests get your heart rate up, and that's what makes you sweat through your shirt. Slow lifting with a minute or more of rest still builds strength without wrecking your afternoon. So skip the jump squats today. Hold dumbbells and lower for a three-count instead.

Save the sweaty stuff for days with no afternoon meetings.
