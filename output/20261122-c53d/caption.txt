A quick bicep curl fix for your dumbbell workout.

If your hips rock forward on every rep, your back is lifting the weight. Stand with your back flat to a wall, keep your elbows pinned at your sides, and alternate arms slowly. Drop a weight or two. The wall keeps you honest.
